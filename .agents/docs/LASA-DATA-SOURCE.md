## Prototype LASA Levels

The current Duoclongo level system is a prototype learning structure.

Levels must NOT be interpreted as:
- LASA prevalence
- frequency
- clinical importance
- severity
- safety risk
- real-world commonness

We currently do not have sufficient research to support those classifications.

For the current prototype, levels may contain sample LASA relationships solely to test:
- lesson progression
- level unlocking
- question generation
- XP
- completion
- progress tracking
- review
- UI/UX

The current sample LASA data is development data and must not be presented as a research-backed ranking.

When authoritative LASA research becomes available:

Research determines which LASA relationships are valid.

Educational goals determine which verified relationships should be taught first.

The level structure should therefore remain easy to replace without requiring a major rewrite of the learning engine.
